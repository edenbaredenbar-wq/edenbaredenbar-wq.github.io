🗺️ How to play

  • 🏙️Everyone starts in Tel Aviv. First to Eilat 🐠 triggers the Final Sprint!
  • 🎲On your turn, draw a challenge for your next stop. You have 30 seconds.
  • ✅Correct = +10 points and your vehicle drives on.
  • 🦹Wrong = you stay, and the next team can steal for +5.
  • 🎴After a turn, an Event Card might appear: traffic jams, falafel, camels, hitchhikes, Bamba…
  • ⚡Final Sprint: buzz in first for +20. Most points wins!
