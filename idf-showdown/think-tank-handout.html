Topic 1

October 7, 2023

BackgroundOn October 7, 2023, the holiday of Simchat Torah, Hamas terrorists from Gaza attacked southern Israel. About 1,200 people were killed and 251 were taken hostage into Gaza: the deadliest day for Jews since the Holocaust. Within days, about 360,000 reservists reported for duty, some flying home from the United States. The war in Gaza that followed caused enormous loss of life and destruction there, and it is debated around the world. A ceasefire began in October 2025, and the living hostages came home.

Thousands of reservists showed up before they were even called. What makes people volunteer like that? Would you?

Topic 2

Should everyone serve?

BackgroundFor decades, ultra-Orthodox (Haredi) men who study Torah full-time were exempt from the draft. In June 2024, Israel's Supreme Court ruled that the government must draft them like everyone else. It's one of the biggest debates in Israel today.

Should full-time religious students be drafted?

Some sayEveryone should share the burden equally, especially in wartime.
Others sayTorah study protects the Jewish people too, and army life clashes with a strictly religious life.

Topic 3

Women in combat

BackgroundSince a law passed in 2000, women can serve in many combat jobs: as pilots, in mixed battalions like Caracal, and in border-defense tank crews, which fought on October 7. Many infantry brigades are still for men only.

Should every unit be open to anyone who passes the tests?

Some sayEqual tests, equal chances: the best person should get the job.
Others sayPhysical demands and each unit's needs should decide, not fairness alone.
