BackgroundFor decades, ultra-Orthodox (Haredi) men who study Torah full-time were exempt from the draft. In June 2024, Israel's Supreme Court ruled that the government must draft them like everyone else. It's one of the biggest debates in Israel today.

Should full-time religious students be drafted?
